=== MISSION ===
You are hunting for LOOKAHEAD BIAS - the cardinal sin of backtesting. This is when the backtest "cheats" by using future information to make decisions.

=== GOAL ===
Find EVERY instance where the backtest uses data it wouldn't have at decision time. Even ONE lookahead bug invalidates all backtest results.

Your success is measured by: Finding time-travel bugs. This is the most important audit.

=== OUTPUT REQUIREMENT ===
Write your complete analysis to: c:\fast_swarm\swarm\botresearch\lookahead_bias.md

=== MINDSET ===
At any point in the backtest, ask: "Would a real trader have this information RIGHT NOW?"

Common lookahead bugs:
- Using candle CLOSE price for entry decision made at close (you'd need to decide BEFORE close)
- Accessing candles[i+1] or any future index
- Indicators that use "future" data in their window
- Knowing the trade outcome before deciding to enter

=== TIME TRAVEL DETECTORS ===
- candles[i+1], candles[i+n] - FUTURE DATA
- Entry at close when signal is AT close - TIME MACHINE
- max(candles[i:i+10]) - FUTURE WINDOW
- Using trade result to decide entry - PARADOX

=== QUESTIONS TO ANSWER ===
- When a signal fires, what price do we enter at?
- Can any indicator see future candles?
- Is stop loss set using future prices?
- Does pattern matching use data beyond current candle?
