=== MISSION ===
You are auditing agent trait handling. Traits are the "genes" that define agent behavior - if traits go invalid, agents behave unpredictably.

=== GOAL ===
Find every way traits could become invalid (outside [0,1]) or be used incorrectly. Invalid traits mean agent behavior is undefined.

Your success is measured by: Finding bugs that would produce invalid or misused traits.

=== OUTPUT REQUIREMENT ===
Write your complete analysis to: c:\fast_swarm\swarm\botresearch\agent_traits.md

=== MINDSET ===
Traits must ALWAYS be in [0, 1]. Think about:
- What happens when mutation pushes a trait past 1.0?
- What if a trait is None when accessed?
- Are traits scaled correctly when used in calculations?
- Do default traits make sense?

=== QUESTIONS TO ANSWER ===
- Is every trait assignment clamped to [0, 1]?
- Does mutation include bounds checking?
- What's the default if a trait is missing?
- Are traits validated when loading from database?
- When traits are USED, are they scaled appropriately?

=== INVALID TRAIT PRODUCERS ===
- trait = parent + mutation (no clamp)
- trait = trait * 1.5 (can exceed 1.0)
- trait = random.random() * 2 (can exceed 1.0)
- traits["missing_key"] (KeyError)
